### 🗂️ All Projects

#### 🧪 Experiments

- **A/B Testing Methodology Toolkit** — 18 A/B methodology modules, each calibrated by simulation: an A/A null check holds Type I error at α, and power curves show the effect size each method can actually detect. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/ab_test) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/ab/)
- **Browser Mini-Games — Analytics Arcade** — 10 playable mini-games as self-contained SVGs: 7 analytics (A/B to p<0.05, funnel drop, cohort catch, retention day) and 3 arcade. One file per game, zero dependencies, no build. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/browser-mini-games) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/games/)
- **Causal / Uplift — CUPED and Individual Treatment Effects** — CUPED cut the standard error by 26% — the same power on 5.5k users per arm instead of 10k. At n=6000 uplift metrics stay noise-dominated: QINI and AUUC cannot separate an oracle from random. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/causal-uplift) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/causal/)
- **Volta Neobank — Product Analytics** — Fixed a neobank's onboarding bottleneck with an A/B test: +5.72pp KYC conversion, €656K/year. 22 projects: funnel → A/B → retention → segmentation → Market & Jobs → RAT v2 → causal. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/volta-banking) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/)

#### 📊 Analytics

- **Churn Prediction — Leakage-Free Retention Model** — A leakage-free churn model: recall@top-10% of 0.53 and 3.07x lift at 0.904 ROC-AUC, on a chronological split with no future activity leaking into features. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/churn-prediction) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/churn/)
- **Cohort Analysis Dashboard** — A triangular cohort retention and LTV matrix on synthetic data: per-cohort ARPU and LTV with an observation-age caveat, plus a Tableau-ready CSV and Hyper export. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/tableau_cohort_analysis) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/cohort/)
- **LinkedIn Content Analytics** — Analytics on my own LinkedIn posts: one parser, four renderers. Formats are compared with confidence intervals — and noise is called noise, not a ranking. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/linkedin-nikita-boyarkin) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/linkedin/)
- **ML Fundamentals — scikit-learn Pipeline** — A leak-free scikit-learn pipeline: missing-data imputation, categorical encoding, a deterministic split and feature scaling, all in one ColumnTransformer fit on the training split only. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/ml) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/ml/)
- **Python Analytics Playground** — ≥80% pytest coverage on a module-based Python analytics toolkit: loading, cleaning, EDA and visualization assembled into one reusable pipeline — the base I copy into every new analysis. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/python) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/python/)
- **RAG Evaluation Harness — retrieval and generation metrics** — A RAG pipeline with a real offline evaluation harness: recall@5 and MRR = 1.00 over 20 labeled questions, faithfulness 1.00, correctness 0.37. TF-IDF retrieval, runs with zero API keys. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/rag-eval-harness) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/rag/)
- **RFM Analysis of Bank Clients** — Segmented a bank's clients into 4 RFM groups and showed that a small high-value share drives most revenue. Marketing moved from mass sends to targeted scenarios. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/rfm-analysis-of-bank-clients) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/rfm/)
- **Sales Calls Analytics Dashboard** — A Streamlit dashboard for AI sales calls on 16,891 synthetic calls: a 4-step funnel (greeting → offer → meeting → qualification), leak breakdown by type, and transcript drill-down. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/sales-calls-dashboard) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/sales-calls/)
- **SERP Rank Lab** — Metasearch SERP analytics lab: inverse-propensity weighting corrects position bias, NDCG and revenue per search disagree, and always-valid mSPRT holds the false-positive rate under repeated looks. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/serp-rank-lab) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/serp-rank/)
- **SQL Analytics Case Study** — 26 SQL cases: 25 on a synthetic ~183k-event dataset + 1 real-data case on UCI Online Retail II. Funnel, retention, LTV, attribution, anomalies, dbt layer on DuckDB, live report on GitHub Pages. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/sql-analytics-case-study) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/sql/)

<summary><b>🏦 Volta Neobank — 23 case studies</b></summary>

- **Volta — Funnel Analysis** — The neobank onboarding funnel: KYC is the bottleneck at 56.6% step conversion, registration loses the most in absolute terms (2,682). Broken down by channel and platform. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-funnel) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/funnel/)
- **Volta — KYC Progress-Bar A/B Test** — A KYC progress bar lifted conversion +5.72pp (Z=5.82, p<0.0001) against a +5pp MDE. CUPED, AA-test and Bonferroni protect the conclusion; +€656K/yr at 44× ROI. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-ab) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/ab/)
- **Volta — Retention & Cohorts** — Cohort triangles showed a step-change after the KYC fix: M1 +11.8pp, M3 +9.2pp and +€227K/yr LTV. Premium LTV is 4.3× Free. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-retention) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/retention/)
- **Volta — User Segmentation** — KMeans with data-driven K=4: Power 12% drive 41% of revenue, 68% drive 92%. Cross-segment migration is worth up to +€310K/yr. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-segmentation) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/segmentation/)
- **Volta — Churn Prediction** — Random Forest adds +0.03 ROC-AUC over logistic regression; the top churn driver is device-error rate (23.7%), not balance or activity. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-churn) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/churn/)
- **Volta — RFM Analysis** — R/F/M scoring 1–5 splits the base into lifecycle segments from Champions to Lost; recency and monetary diverge — 'frequent but cheap' and 'rare but large'. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-rfm) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/rfm/)
- **Volta — CLV Modeling** — Three lifetime-value methods: historical, retention-curve and Gamma-Gamma. The order Power > Growth > Casual > Dormant is robust across all methods. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-clv) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/clv/)
- **Volta — Marketing Attribution** — First-touch, last-touch, linear and Shapley attribution. Shapley (data-driven) reallocates budget and leads with referral; the conclusion is robust to model choice. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-attribution) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/attribution/)
- **Volta — Anomaly Detection** — Isolation Forest has the best F1 (50.4%), catching amount, night-hour and frequency anomalies. Z-score is precise but cautious; the comparison of the three detectors ran against ground truth. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-anomalies) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/anomalies/)
- **Volta — Spend Analysis** — Spend breakdown by category and channel: bills (25.8%) and travel (20.2%) make up nearly half the turnover, groceries is the most frequent category. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-spend) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/spend/)
- **Volta — Support & Churn** — Churn rises with ticket count: 37.1% at zero contacts vs 81.1% at 3+. Support is a measurable retention lever, not just a cost center. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-support-churn) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/support-churn/)
- **Volta — NPS Trends** — Monthly NPS hovers near zero; the strongest drivers are app quality (+30.3) and product (+29.4), the main source of dissatisfaction is fees (−59.4). · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-nps) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/nps/)
- **Volta — JTBD × Cohorts** — Job segments and behavioral cohorts are not independent (chi² p<0.001): Dormant concentrates in Digital Newcomers 45+ (39.4%) vs Family Budgeters (15.1%). · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-jtbd) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/jtbd/)
- **Volta — Traveler Unit Economics** — Travelers lose €0.45 per €100 FX transaction; break-even needs FX cost cut from 1.00% to 0.55%, otherwise the loss grows with volume. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-unit-economics) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/unit-economics/)
- **Volta — Premium Upsell** — Free→Premium conversion concentrates in the anchor (17.3%) and status-seekers (41.2%); Digital Newcomers 45+ convert just 1.8% — the value prop doesn't transfer. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-premium-upsell) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/premium-upsell/)
- **Volta — 45+ KYC Deep-Dive** — Age-sliced A/B HTE: 35–44 +11.0pp and 18–24 +5.3pp, but 45+ +1.4pp (ns). Referral (trust) converts 45+ best — friction is trust, not UX. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-kyc-45) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/kyc-45/)
- **Volta — Referral Segments** — Referral funnel by JTBD segment: anchor 29.6% vs Digital Newcomers 45+ 4.8% and families 8.6%. The gap opens at accept, not KYC. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-referral) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/referral/)
- **Volta — Assisted CAC vs LTV** — Does the 45+ trust track pay off: 45+ LTV/CAC = 0.66 against a ≥3 gate; assisted CAC €120 is ~3× referral and doesn't pay back (50-month payback). · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-assisted-cac) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/assisted-cac/)
- **Volta — FX Sourcing Feasibility** — The 0.55% gate is reachable only at SOM scale (~€332M/mo, 122× today) — a cold-start, not 'impossible'. Best quote is Interbank Prime at 0.745%. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-fx-sourcing) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/fx-sourcing/)
- **Volta — Segment Premium Offers** — A/B: a segment offer lifts gap segments (+3.3pp 45+, +4.2pp families, +3.8pp travelers) — Holm-significant, but the anchor barely moves. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-premium-offers) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/premium-offers/)
- **Volta — Anchor Launch CAC at Scale** — LTV/CAC ≥3 holds only to ~70K users; at SOM it falls to 1.76× and a 17-month payback. The constraint is cheap-channel capacity, not budget. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-anchor-cac) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/anchor-cac/)
- **Volta — Dormant 45+ Win-back** — Three-arm win-back: human +5.34pp and light-touch +2.77pp vs control. Light-touch pays off at 30–90d (ROI 2.54 / 1.26), human ROI 0.40 — kill as a mass channel. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-dormant-winback) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/dormant-winback/)
- **Volta — Causal Validation of KYC (DiD)** — A DiD test shows the KYC fix causally lifted M3 retention by +9.09pp (95% CI [+6.21, +11.96]) — flat pre-trends, placebo ≈ 0, max |SMD| 0.157 < 0.2. · [Repo](https://github.com/NikitaBoyarkin/case-causal-kyc) · [Case study](https://nikitaboyarkin.github.io/projects/volta/causal-kyc/)
